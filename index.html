TokenNest

Fractional Property Ownership in Baltics, Nordics & EU – Demo for Investors

Concept

TokenNest allows investors to buy fractional ownership in specific properties across Baltic, Nordic and EU countries, starting from as low as €50.

Unlike traditional REITs, investors know exactly which property they own. Owners and developers can list properties to raise funds, while investors can buy, sell, or swap their shares.

Fees & Wallet (Concept)

Wallet: Investors hold credits equivalent to euros. All transactions are done using these credits.

Fees:

  • 2% annual fee on Assets Under Management (AUM).
  • 0.5% commission on investor profit.
  • 0.2% fee on each buy/sell transaction.

*This demo does not process real payments. In production, these fees would be calculated and applied via a secure backend.

Swap & Liquidity (Concept)

Investors can sell their shares, swap with other investors or developers, and pay or receive the difference if the property values are not equal.

This creates a liquid marketplace for fractional property ownership, while keeping minimum investment as low as €50 for individuals and €1,500 for organisations.
